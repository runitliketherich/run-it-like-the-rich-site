The ABC framework

Three letters every owner needs, in order.

A

Admin & Operations

Files, passwords, workflows, SOPs and the weekly, monthly and yearly rhythms that keep things moving.

B

Books

Clean books, real reconciliations and reporting you can actually decide from.

C

Compliance

Filings, licenses, payroll and the deadlines that cost money when they're missed.

The Business Reset

Five R's to fix it while it's running.

  1. Reveal

    See what's really happening in the business, not what you hope is happening.

  2. Reframe

    Decide what matters. Edit the plan, not the mission.

  3. Rebuild

    Put the Admin, Books and Compliance systems in place.

  4. Refine

    Tighten what works. Automate the ordinary.

  5. Repeat

    Run the rhythm every week, month and year. Process beats panic.
