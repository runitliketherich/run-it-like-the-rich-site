Why start early

You can't manufacture years of clean history in six months.

Serious buyers look at multi-year trends. They want consistent books, documented procedures and proof the team runs the business without you. None of that can be rushed in the months before a sale.

Starting early gives you time to fix margin leaks, build clean comparable books, and prove the business works while you step back.

No one can honestly promise you a higher multiple. What we can do is go after the things buyers use to discount your earnings and add risk.
